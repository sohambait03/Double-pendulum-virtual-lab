Theory

  • Links are rigid and massless.
  • Masses are concentrated at the ends of the links.
  • Joints are frictionless.
  • Air resistance is neglected.
  • Motion is planar.
  • Gravitational acceleration is constant.

Theory & Equations

Generalized Coordinates

The angles θ₁ and θ₂ are measured from the downward vertical.

$$ x_1=l_1\sin(\theta_1) $$
$$ y_1=-l_1\cos(\theta_1) $$
$$ x_2= l_1\sin(\theta_1) + l_2\sin(\theta_2) $$
$$ y_2= -l_1\cos(\theta_1) - l_2\cos(\theta_2) $$

Kinetic Energy

$$ T= \frac{1}{2}(m_1+m_2)l_1^2\dot{\theta}_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}m_2l_2^2\dot{\theta}_2^2 + m_2l_1l_2 \dot{\theta}_1\dot{\theta}_2 \cos(\theta_1-\theta_2) $$

Potential Energy

$$ V= -(m_1+m_2)gl_1\cos(\theta_1) - m_2gl_2\cos(\theta_2) $$

Total Energy

$$ E=T+V $$

Equations of Motion

$$ M(\theta)\ddot{\theta}=b $$
