Theory

System Assumptions

  • Links are rigid and massless.
  • Masses are concentrated at the ends of the links.
  • Joints are frictionless.
  • Air resistance is neglected.
  • Motion is planar.
  • Gravitational acceleration is constant.

Generalized Coordinates

The angles θ₁ and θ₂ are measured from the downward vertical.

$$ x_1=l_1\sin(\theta_1) $$
$$ y_1=-l_1\cos(\theta_1) $$
$$ x_2= l_1\sin(\theta_1) + l_2\sin(\theta_2) $$
$$ y_2= -l_1\cos(\theta_1) - l_2\cos(\theta_2) $$

Kinetic Energy

$$ T= \frac{1}{2}(m_1+m_2)l_1^2\dot{\theta}_1^2 + \frac{1}{2}m_2l_2^2\dot{\theta}_2^2 + m_2l_1l_2 \dot{\theta}_1\dot{\theta}_2 \cos(\theta_1-\theta_2) $$

Potential Energy

$$ V= -(m_1+m_2)gl_1\cos(\theta_1) - m_2gl_2\cos(\theta_2) $$

Total Mechanical Energy

$$ E=T+V $$

Equations of Motion

The nonlinear equations are solved numerically using fourth-order Runge-Kutta integration.

$$ M(\theta)\ddot{\theta}=b $$

Experimental Procedure

  1. Set the length of link 1.
  2. Set the length of link 2.
  3. Set mass m₁.
  4. Set mass m₂.
  5. Set the initial angle θ₁.
  6. Set the initial angle θ₂.
  7. Set gravitational acceleration.
  8. Select the desired simulation speed.
  9. Click Start.
  10. Observe the motion of both pendulum links.
  11. Observe angular displacement, angular velocity and angular acceleration.
  12. Observe the energy conservation behaviour.
  13. Analyze the generated graphs.
  14. Download the observation data.
