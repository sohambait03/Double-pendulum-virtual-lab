Theory

System Assumptions

  • Links are rigid and massless.
  • Masses are concentrated at the ends of the links.
  • Joints are frictionless.
  • Air resistance is neglected.
  • Motion is planar.
  • Gravitational acceleration is constant.

Coordinates

The angles θ₁ and θ₂ are measured from the downward vertical.

$$ x_1=l_1\sin(\theta_1) $$
$$ y_1=-l_1\cos(\theta_1) $$
$$ x_2=l_1\sin(\theta_1) +l_2\sin(\theta_2) $$
$$ y_2=-l_1\cos(\theta_1) -l_2\cos(\theta_2) $$

Energy

The total mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic and potential energy.

$$ E=T+V $$

Equations of Motion

The nonlinear equations of motion are obtained using the Lagrangian formulation.

$$ M(\theta)\ddot{\theta}=b $$

Experimental Procedure

  1. Set the link lengths using the sliders.
  2. Set the masses of both pendulum bobs.
  3. Set the initial angles θ₁ and θ₂.
  4. Set gravitational acceleration.
  5. Click Start.
  6. Observe the nonlinear motion.
  7. Observe θ, ω and α values.
  8. Monitor total mechanical energy.
  9. Compare the plots generated by the simulation.
  10. Download the observation data as CSV.
